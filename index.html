Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

Lapline runs in your browser. Your workouts are opened and saved right there, on your phone or computer. There’s no Lapline server and no account, so there’s nowhere for your data to go.

Switching browser or computer? Export a backup and restore it there.

This website counts visits with SimpleAnalytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. The app only does if you turn it on.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Lapline is free and open source under the MIT licence. There’s no paid tier and nothing to sign up for.

Which devices does it work with?

Anything that records .fit or .gpx files: Garmin, COROS, Wahoo, Suunto, Polar, Zwift, bike computers, and Apple Watch through an export app such as HealthFit. .tcx files aren’t supported yet.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, on your device. Each browser keeps its own copy, so a different browser or computer starts empty: use Settings → Backup to export everything as one file and restore it there. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open Lapline in your phone’s browser and add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone and iPad this also matters for your data: Safari deletes a website’s saved data after 7 days without a visit unless it’s installed.

Is this medical advice?

No. VO₂ max, training effect, recovery time and training load are estimates for training guidance. They can be wrong and can’t diagnose anything. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor before changing your training.

Does it sync with Garmin Connect or Strava?

No, on purpose: that would need a server and an account. You bring the files. In Chrome or Edge on a computer you can also connect a Garmin watch’s USB folder once, and from then on one click brings in anything new.
